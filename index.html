        Sources & method

        How this dashboard is built

        Chalkline is compiled by a scheduled monthly research task and reviewed for accuracy before publication. sources are cited in this edition.

        Selection rules

        • Primary sources first: federal statistics (NCES, NAEP, Census), peer-reviewed or working-paper research, and nationally representative surveys (RAND, Pew, Gallup).
        • Trade press (Education Week, K-12 Dive, Chalkbeat) is cited for news and trackers, never as the origin of a statistic when the primary source is public.
        • Every number carries the population, the date and the survey window it applies to; changing definitions are flagged in notes.

        Reading the figures

        • NAEP scores are rounded; NCES computes reported changes from unrounded values, so a difference of rounded scores can differ by a point.
        • Survey estimates carry sampling error (typically ±2 to ±4 points). Comparisons across different surveys are labeled as such.
        • School years are labeled by their spring (2025 means 2024–25) unless stated.

        Update cadence

        The task protocol, data schema and validator live in the repository: source on GitHub. Data files: overview, AI, math, evidence register, briefing, sources.
